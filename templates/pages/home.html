{# ============ ADMISSIONS ============ #}
Admissions

Start Your Journey With Us

Admission into the College follows a simple, transparent process. New applicants are admitted through our dedicated Admission Portal; once admitted, every other academic activity is managed right here in the College Management System.

  1. 1
    Submit Application
    Complete the online application form and pay the application fee.
  2. 2
    Screening & Admission
    Shortlisted candidates are screened and offered admission by the Registrar.
  3. 3
    Registration
    Accept admission, pay tuition, and register courses through your student portal.
Apply Now Download Brochure

Online applications open soon — the buttons above are placeholders.

Admission Requirements (Typical)
  • Five (5) SSCE/NECO/NABTEB credits including English and Mathematics
  • Valid JAMB UTME result meeting the department's cut-off mark
  • Completed online application and payment of application fee
  • Valid means of identification
  • Attendance at screening/interview, where applicable
